Taking honey home to the USA yourself

If you are visiting Jamaica, you can buy Hey Hon honey here and take it home. Honey is generally allowed into the United States in commercial packaging for personal consumption, and it must be declared to US Customs. Pack honey in checked luggage, because liquids over 3.4 oz (100 ml) are not allowed in carry-on luggage. Check current US Customs and FDA rules before you travel, because rules can change.

A note on business shipping to the USA

A business sending food to the United States must file FDA prior notice before the food arrives. That is one reason USA orders are handled by WhatsApp quote, not by automatic checkout, until our USA shipping route is confirmed.
