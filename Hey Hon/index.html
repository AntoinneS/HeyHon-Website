Frequently Asked

Where are your apiaries located?

All our hives are located deep in the rural, pristine hills of St. Mary, Jamaica, far away from commercial agriculture and pollution.

What makes cold extracted raw honey superior?

Commercial honey is often heavily processed and pasteurized, stripping away natural floral aromas, beneficial enzymes, and antioxidants. Our cold extracted raw honey preserves every nuance of St. Mary's wildflower terroir, delivering unmatched depth, aroma, and vitality.

What is Whipped Honey?

Whipped (or creamed) honey is pure raw honey that has been gently mixed in a controlled process to form very small crystals. It contains nothing but 100% honey, yielding a smooth, buttery, spreadable texture that never sets hard.

How should I store my Hey Hon?

Store at room temperature in a dry cupboard. Never refrigerate raw honey. If your regular raw honey crystallizes over time, simply place the glass jar in warm water.
