Frequently asked questions

How much is delivery in Kingston, Portmore, and Spanish Town?

Portmore courier delivery is JMD $500. Kingston and Spanish Town courier delivery is JMD $800. Kingston delivery is free on orders over JMD $6,000 subtotal.

Where can I pick up my order for free?

Free pickup is available at Shop 15, Northside Plaza, Kingston 6 (Liguanea); Sovereign Village, Portmore; and Digicel Head Office. Select your preferred location at checkout and confirm your pickup window on WhatsApp.

How does Knutsford Express delivery work?

Choose your parish under Knutsford Express ($700 Flat Rate) at checkout and enter your preferred station (such as Drax Hall, Mandeville, Montego Bay, or Savanna-la-Mar). We confirm your dispatch day and receipt details on WhatsApp.

How much does Hey Hon Raw Honey cost?

Raw St. Mary Wildflower Honey is JMD $2,200 for a 266ml glass jar and JMD $3,200 for a 500ml glass jar.
