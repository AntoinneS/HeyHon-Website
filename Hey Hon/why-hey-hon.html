The Hey Hon Difference

Why settle for ordinary when you can experience the extraordinary?

Pure, unheated wildflower nectar harvested from single origin hives in the hills of St. Mary, Jamaica.

A Symphony of Tropical Blooms

The flavor of a truly great honey is written by the landscape it comes from. Our bees forage freely across the pristine, pesticide free hills of St. Mary, gathering nectar from a remarkably diverse tapestry of wild flora.

This vibrant ecosystem creates a deeply layered flavor profile that mass produced commercial brands simply cannot replicate. Every single spoonful offers a rich, velvety texture and complex aromatic notes that are completely unique to our island.

Pure Vitality in Every Drop

For those who value authentic, unrefined food, purity is non-negotiable. We believe in preserving exactly what the bees create. Our gentle, cold extracted process ensures that every jar remains entirely raw and unheated.

Because we never expose our harvest to high temperatures, your honey arrives with its natural enzymes, bee pollen, propolis, and wildflower aroma intact. This is not just a sweetener; it is Jamaican honey in its purest form.

Strictly Governed by Nature

We do not force our harvests to meet commercial demands. Our yield is strictly limited and entirely dependent on the natural blooming cycles of the Jamaican hills.

This means every batch is rare, unique, and available only while nature allows. Once a season's harvest is gone, you must patiently wait for the next. This exclusivity ensures that when you secure a jar of Hey Hon, you are holding a fleeting piece of the environment in your hands.
