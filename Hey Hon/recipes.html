Aesthetic Sips

Iced Strawberry Matcha with Honey Cloud Foam

A visually stunning, vibrant morning ritual. We replace processed syrups with our signature raw honey to create a perfectly balanced, earthy and sweet masterpiece that looks just as incredible as it tastes.

What you need

  • 2 tablespoons Hey Hon Raw Honey
  • 1 teaspoon ceremonial grade matcha powder
  • 2 ounces hot water
  • 1/4 cup fresh strawberries pureed
  • 3/4 cup oat milk or milk of choice
  • 2 tablespoons heavy cream or coconut cream
  • Ice cubes

How to craft it

  1. Whisk your matcha powder with hot water until beautifully frothy and completely smooth.
  2. In a separate small bowl vigorously whisk your cream with one tablespoon of Hey Hon raw honey until it forms a soft fluffy cloud.
  3. Spoon the fresh strawberry puree into the bottom of a tall glass and add a generous handful of ice.
  4. Pour in your oat milk followed by the remaining tablespoon of raw honey.
  5. Gently pour the vibrant green matcha over the milk to create a gorgeous layered effect.
  6. Spoon the sweet honey cloud foam over the top and enjoy immediately.
Morning Rituals

Raw Honey Shaken Espresso

Transform your daily caffeine fix into a cafe quality indulgence. The intense heat of fresh espresso melts our raw honey instantly before being shaken over ice for a rich frothy finish.

What you need

  • 1 generous tablespoon Hey Hon Raw Honey
  • 2 shots freshly pulled espresso
  • A splash of creamy oat milk
  • A pinch of flaky sea salt
  • Ice cubes

How to craft it

  1. Add the Raw Honey to the bottom of a cocktail shaker or mason jar.
  2. Pour the freshly pulled hot espresso directly over the honey and swirl gently until it melts together.
  3. Fill the shaker to the brim with ice cubes and add the pinch of flaky sea salt.
  4. Secure the lid and shake vigorously for fifteen seconds until deeply chilled and frothy.
  5. Pour the entire mixture including the ice into a beautiful glass.
  6. Top with a splash of oat milk and watch the elegant marbling effect before taking your first sip.
Savory Cravings

Ricotta & Chili Raw Honey Artisan Toast

The perfect balance of creamy savory and sweet heat. We take creamy ricotta, spread it thick over toasted sourdough, and finish it with a generous drizzle of chili-infused Hey Hon Raw Honey.

What you need

  • 2 tablespoons Hey Hon Raw Honey
  • 1 pinch crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 thick slice fresh artisan sourdough bread
  • 1/3 cup whole milk ricotta cheese
  • Fresh figs or seasonal fruits
  • Flaky sea salt and fresh thyme

How to craft it

  1. In a small dish stir the crushed red pepper flakes into the raw honey and let it sit to infuse its heat.
  2. Toast your sourdough slice in a pan with a touch of olive oil until perfectly golden and crisp.
  3. Beat the ricotta cheese with a fork until fluffy then spread it generously edge to edge over the warm toast.
  4. Layer the fresh figs beautifully across the ricotta base.
  5. Drizzle the chilli raw honey liberally over the entire toast.
  6. Finish with a sprinkle of flaky sea salt and fresh thyme leaves for a gorgeous culinary experience.
