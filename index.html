Frequently Asked

Where is Hey Hon honey produced in Jamaica?

Hey Hon honey is sustainably harvested from single origin apiaries located in the pristine, rural hills of St. Mary, Jamaica.

Is Hey Hon honey pure and raw?

Yes. Hey Hon honey is 100% pure, raw, cold extracted honey from single origin apiaries in St. Mary, Jamaica, with no added sugar and no high heat processing. Commercial honey is often heavily processed and pasteurized, which strips away natural floral aromas, beneficial enzymes, and antioxidants.

Why does raw honey crystallize, and how do I soften it?

Crystallization is natural for real raw honey. It does not mean the honey has gone bad. Raw honey crystallizes over time, especially in cooler conditions. Place the glass jar in warm water until the honey softens. Do not use boiling water or a microwave. Store at room temperature in a dry cupboard.

Is honey safe for infants?

No. Do not give honey to infants under 12 months.
